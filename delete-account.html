Delete your scoi account

You can delete your scoi account and the personal data we hold about you. This page explains how.

Delete in the app (recommended)

  1. Open the scoi app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Open Privacy & data.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion is immediate. You cannot recover the account afterwards.

Request by email

If you cannot use the app, email kontakt.scoi@proton.me from the address linked to your account. We will verify the request and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in credentials
  • Your profile (display name)
  • Favorites and selected interests
  • Push notification tokens and notification log

What may remain for a limited time

  • Encrypted database backups (up to 30 days, Supabase)
  • Crash reports in Sentry (we aim for no more than 90 days; not tied to your account after deletion)

More information

See our Privacy Policy or contact kontakt.scoi@proton.me.
