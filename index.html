Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sina Sultani (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use the scoi mobile application (the “App”).

We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with the EU GDPR, the Swedish Data Protection Act, and equivalent laws where you may use the App.

1. Data Controller

  • Sina Sultani
  • Address: Såggatan 50A, 41467, Gothenburg, Sweden
  • Email: kontakt.scoi@proton.me

2. What data we collect

2.1 Account data

  • Email address — account, sign-in emails, recovery.
  • Display name — from your profile.
  • About — optional bio.
  • Profile photo — optional. Stored in a public bucket under your user id with a random filename. Anyone with the URL can open it. Replacing the photo deletes the previous file.

Legal basis: contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

2.2 App content

  • Favorites — events and places you save, with a snapshot at save time.
  • Interests — categories you select.

2.3 Push notifications

  • Expo push token — with platform, timezone, and locale, after you allow notifications.
  • Notification log — which reminders we already sent, so we do not send the same one twice.

Legal basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a)). Revoke in OS settings; the next signed-in launch marks the token inactive.

2.4 Location

Approximate GPS is read while you use nearby features (weather, map, bikes, city activities, public transport). We do not keep a location history on your account. Coordinates are sent to the services in section 4 for that request, and may sit in a short backend cache (minutes). Opening directions sends a destination (and typically your position) to Apple Maps or Google Maps.

Legal basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a)).

2.5 On-device preferences

Language and guest mode stay on the device, not in our database.

2.6 Crash reports

Sentry may receive a stack trace, device model, OS, app version, and a random install id when a crash key is in the build. We disable default collection of IP, email, and user id. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).

2.7 Authentication metadata

Supabase Auth keeps created-at, last sign-in, and briefly sign-in IP. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).

3. Data we do not collect

  • We do not sell data, use it for ads, or share it with brokers.
  • We do not store a timeline of where you have been.
  • We do not read contacts, calendar, or microphone. Photos only if you pick a profile picture.

6. Your rights

  • Access / portability — Export account, profile, and favorites in the App. The file does not include push tokens or the notification log. Email us for a fuller copy.
  • Rectification — edit your profile.
  • Erasure — delete account in the App.
  • Restrict, object, withdraw consent — OS settings and/or kontakt.scoi@proton.me (we respond within 30 days).
  • Complaint — Sweden: imy.se.

7. Security

HTTPS in transit; hashed passwords; row-level security; service-role key not shipped in the App; avatars publicly readable by URL; sensitive endpoints rate-limited. Breach notification follows GDPR Arts. 33–34.

8. Children

General-audience app. We do not verify age. If a child has an account, email us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

We update the date at the top. There is no separate in-app alert. If you disagree, delete your account and stop using the App.
