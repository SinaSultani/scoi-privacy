Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sina Sultani (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use the scoi mobile application (the “App”).

We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Swedish Data Protection Act, and equivalent laws in other jurisdictions where you may use the App.

1. Data Controller

The data controller for the personal data collected through the App is:

  • Sina Sultani
  • Address: Såggatan 50A, 41467, Gothenburg, Sweden
  • Email: kontakt.scoi@proton.me

2. What data we collect

We only collect data that is necessary to run the App.

2.1 Account data

  • Email address — used to create and identify your account, to send you sign-in confirmation emails, and to recover access.
  • Display name — what you type as your name when creating your profile.
  • Profile photo (avatar) — optional. Stored as an image file with an unguessable filename.

Source: provided directly by you when you sign up or edit your profile.
Legal basis: performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

2.2 App content

  • Favorites — the list of events and places you have marked as favorite, with a snapshot of the event/place data at the time of marking.
  • Interests — categories you have selected (e.g. food, music, nature) to personalise the feed.

Source: provided by you through your actions in the App.
Legal basis: performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

2.3 Push notification data

  • Expo push token — an opaque identifier issued by Expo's push notification service that lets us deliver notifications to your device. Stored alongside your platform (iOS / Android), timezone, and locale.

Source: collected from the device only after you grant push-notification permission.
Legal basis: consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can revoke at any time in your OS settings.

2.4 Approximate location

Coarse device location (latitude/longitude) is used to surface nearby weather, events, and places. We do not store your location history on our servers — it is queried on demand and passed to third-party APIs only for the duration of a request.

Source: device's location services, only after you grant location permission.
Legal basis: consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can revoke at any time in your OS settings.

2.5 Crash reports and diagnostics

Crash reports and unhandled error events, captured via Sentry. Each report contains a stack trace, the device model, OS version, app version, and a randomly-generated installation identifier. We have disabled collection of user-identifying fields (email, user ID) and personally identifying information in error breadcrumbs.

Source: the device, automatically, when an unhandled error occurs.
Legal basis: legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) — keeping the App working.

2.6 Authentication metadata

Supabase Auth automatically retains some technical metadata about your account, including account-creation date, last sign-in time, and (briefly) the IP address used for sign-in attempts. This is used for security and audit purposes. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

3. Data we do not collect

  • We do not sell your data to anyone.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or tracking.
  • We do not share your data with data brokers.
  • We do not collect precise GPS history.
  • We do not read your contacts, calendar, photos library, or microphone except as needed at the moment you actively use a feature (e.g. picking a profile photo).

4. Third parties we share data with

We use a small number of service providers (“processors”) to operate the App. They process data on our behalf, under contract, and may not use it for their own purposes.

Provider What they receive Purpose Location
Supabase Account data, profile, favorites, push tokens Hosting our backend and storing your data EU (eu-north-1 / Stockholm)
Expo Your Expo push token and notification payloads Delivering push notifications United States
Sentry Crash reports, device model, OS, app version Diagnosing crashes and bugs EU and United States
Göteborg Stad APIs The contents of your search queries (no personal data) Source for events and places EU

When data is transferred outside the EU/EEA, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent safeguards approved by the European Commission.

5. How long we keep your data

Data type Retention
Account, profile, favorites, push tokens Until you delete your account.
Crash reports 90 days, then automatically purged by Sentry.
Notification log 30 days.
Backups Up to 30 days as part of Supabase's automated backups.

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Profile → Privacy & data → Delete account inside the App. Deletion is immediate and irreversible.

6. Your rights under GDPR

  • Right of access (Article 15) — In-App: Profile → Privacy & data → Export my data.
  • Right to rectification (Article 16) — most fields can be edited in your profile.
  • Right to erasure (Article 17) — Profile → Privacy & data → Delete account.
  • Right to restrict processing (Article 18).
  • Right to data portability (Article 20) — the JSON export above.
  • Right to object (Article 21).
  • Right to withdraw consent (Article 7(3)) — via your OS settings.
  • Right to lodge a complaint — in Sweden, imy.se.

To exercise any right that isn't available in-App, email us at kontakt.scoi@proton.me and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Security

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS.
  • Passwords are hashed by Supabase Auth; we never see your password in plain text.
  • Server-side data is protected by per-row access controls (RLS).
  • Avatars use unguessable filenames and rotate on every upload.
  • API endpoints are rate-limited to prevent abuse.

If we become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you and the relevant supervisory authority as required by Article 33–34 GDPR.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher threshold). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will notify you in the App and update the “Last updated” date at the top. Continued use of the App after such a change indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For any privacy-related question, complaint, or request, email us at kontakt.scoi@proton.me.
